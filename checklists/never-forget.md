# The never-forget list

Whatever your business is, check these before you go live, and again every few months.

- [ ] **Backups**: the data is backed up automatically, and you have **tested a restore** at least once.
- [ ] **Who has access**: you know who can open every account (code, hosting, payments, email, domain).
- [ ] **Two-factor sign-in** is on for every one of those accounts.
- [ ] **People who leave** lose their access on their last day.
- [ ] **Logs**: when something goes wrong, you can see what happened, and the logs never contain private details.
- [ ] **Secrets** (passwords, API keys, tokens) are kept in a password manager, never in the code and never in a chat with the agent.
- [ ] **A privacy policy and terms of use** are published, and they say what the app really does.
- [ ] **Budget alerts** are set on every paid service, so a mistake cannot cost you a surprise bill.
- [ ] **A plan for a bad day** is written down: who you call, how you tell customers, and how you go back to the last good version.

We come back to this list in the videos on secrets, on privacy, roles and logs, and before going live.
