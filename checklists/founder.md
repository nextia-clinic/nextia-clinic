# The founder checklist

Have these ready before the build starts. Most of it takes one afternoon. The accounts and the budget belong to you, as the owner of the business, not to the agent.

## Your business identity

- [ ] **A domain name** for your business (for example `nextiaclinic.com`), registered in the name of the business, with automatic renewal on.
- [ ] **A business email address on that domain** (for example `hello@nextiaclinic.com`), so that every account belongs to the business and not to a personal email.
- [ ] A short list of the **email addresses the app will need**, for example one for customers, one for payment notices, and one for automatic emails like reminders. Ours: `hello@`, `payments@` and `noreply@nextiaclinic.com`.

## Accounts

- [ ] **Every account the app needs is owned by the business**: code (GitHub), hosting, email, payments, the AI model API. Write the list down, with who can open each one.
- [ ] **A GitHub organisation** for the business, not only a personal account (ours: `nextia-clinic`).
- [ ] **At least two people** can open each important account, or you have a written plan for how someone else gets in if you cannot.
- [ ] Test accounts and real accounts are **kept apart** (for example, payments in test mode while you build).

## Safety

- [ ] **A password manager** holds every password, key and recovery code (we use KeePass). Never in the code, never in a chat with the agent, never in an email.
- [ ] **Two-factor sign-in** is on for every account, and the recovery codes are saved in the password manager.
- [ ] **Budget alerts** are set on every paid service, at the monthly budget from the founder questions, so that a mistake cannot cost you a surprise bill.

## The plan

- [ ] The **founder questions** are answered (`checklists/founder-questions.md`).
- [ ] The **one-page plan** (`docs/plan.md`) exists, and every requirement is approved.
- [ ] The **never-forget list** (`checklists/never-forget.md`) is read, and you know which items come later in the course.
